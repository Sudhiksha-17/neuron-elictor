## Implications (for discussion, nothing changed)
- Scores are not bitwise portable between the A100 and the 4090. The difference is a few bf16 steps (<= ~2%).
  That is below the proposed 1.05 win threshold, but enough to flip the strict `score > fineweb_max` test near ratio 1.0.
  Beat/no-beat comparisons should use FineWeb maxima rescored on the same GPU type (e.g. rescore each pilot's argmax
  context, or its top-10, here), or rely on the 1.05 ratio threshold.
- The phase 1 2% check fails here because of the same cross-kernel noise at a tiny magnitude. The tolerance was not
  relaxed; that is a decision for the team.
